Team Name: Civic Neurons

---

Overview

Civic Neurons is an AI-powered platform that helps users create, optimize, and consume digital content more effectively.  

It combines content generation, engagement scoring, and intelligent news processing to deliver clear, personalized, and trustworthy information.

The platform focuses on clarity, trust, and reducing information overload.

---

Problem

Digital creators and news consumers often face:

- Information overload  
- Clickbait and low-credibility content  
- Lack of personalization  
- Biased or one-sided news  
- No clear feedback on content quality  

---

Solution

Our AI-driven copilot:

- Generates and optimizes content  
- Provides engagement scoring  
- Creates TL;DR summaries  
- Personalizes content based on interests  
- Compares multiple sources to reduce bias  
- Filters clickbait and fake news  

---

Key Features

- AI-powered news summarization (TL;DR)  
- Personalized news feed  
- Bias detection using multi-source comparison  
- Clickbait and fake news filtering  
- Multi-source aggregation for balanced views  
- Smart alerts for important updates  
- Multi-language support  
- Engagement insights and trending topics  

---

How It Works

1. User provides input or selects interests  
2. AI generates or summarizes content  
3. Content optimizer refines clarity and tone  
4. Engagement scoring evaluates quality  
5. Recommendations are provided  
6. Final optimized content is delivered  

---

Tech Stack

Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS  
Backend: FastAPI (Python)  
AI/LLMs: GPT-4 / Gemini  
NLP: spaCy, TextBlob  
Database (optional): PostgreSQL  
Deployment: Vercel + AWS/Render  
Version Control: GitHub  

---

Goal

To enable smarter, faster, and more trustworthy digital content consumption and creation for users across Bharat.
