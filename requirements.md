# **ContentFlow AI — Requirements Document**

---

## **1. Overview**

ContentFlow AI is an AI-powered web platform that helps users create, optimize, and evaluate digital content before publishing.

The platform combines Large Language Models (LLMs) with a transparent rule-based scoring engine to provide actionable, explainable feedback on content quality and engagement potential.

The goal is to enable creators and businesses to make data-backed content decisions instead of relying on guesswork.

---

## **2. Problem Statement**

Digital content creators and businesses often struggle to consistently produce high-performing content.

Key challenges include:

* Difficulty writing engaging content
* Limited clarity on why some posts perform poorly
* Inconsistent tone and structure
* No reliable way to predict engagement before publishing
* Time-consuming manual editing cycles

Most existing tools either *generate content* or *show analytics*, but rarely explain **how to improve content in a measurable and practical way**.

---

## **3. Objectives**

ContentFlow AI aims to:

* Assist users in producing higher-quality content using AI
* Provide engagement scoring using AI + heuristic evaluation
* Offer clear before-and-after improvement insights
* Predict engagement potential in a structured manner
* Deliver explainable feedback rather than black-box results
* Scale efficiently for growing user demand

---

## **4. Target Users**

* Social media creators
* Digital marketing agencies
* Startups and small businesses
* Bloggers and professional writers
* Educators and online trainers

---

## **5. Functional Requirements**

### **5.1 Content Input**

Users must be able to submit:

* Topic
* Draft content
* Platform type (LinkedIn, Instagram, Blog, etc.)
* Target audience

---

### **5.2 AI Content Generation**

The system should:

* Generate an optimized version of the content
* Adjust tone based on audience and platform
* Improve clarity, readability, and flow
* Format content for platform-specific standards

---

### **5.3 Engagement Scoring**

Content evaluation should combine:

* AI-based semantic analysis
* Heuristic rule-based scoring

Outputs must include:

* Engagement probability score
* Readability metrics
* Tone analysis
* Structure quality feedback

---

### **5.4 Explainability**

The platform must provide:

* Before vs. after comparison
* Highlighted improvements
* Short AI-generated explanations for changes

This ensures user trust and learning.

---

### **5.5 Analytics Feedback**

The system should:

* Store past content scores
* Display improvement trends
* Allow version comparison

This supports continuous user improvement.

---

## **6. Non-Functional Requirements**

* Fast response time (under 3–5 seconds)
* Scalable AI processing pipeline
* Secure authentication and data handling
* Cloud-ready architecture
* Lightweight, intuitive UI
* High availability and reliability

---

## **7. Impact & Value**

ContentFlow AI delivers value by:

* Reducing content editing time
* Increasing confidence in publishing
* Helping users understand what makes content effective
* Enabling consistent content quality

This empowers creators and businesses to communicate more effectively.

---

## **8. Success Metrics**

Success will be measured through:

* Increase in predicted engagement scores
* Reduction in content editing time
* Improvement in readability scores
* Higher consistency across published content
* Positive user feedback and adoption
